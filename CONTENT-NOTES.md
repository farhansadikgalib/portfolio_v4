- The 100+ clients / 5-star claim is omitted from the central story because supporting freelance profile links were not supplied.
- MedEx and MedEx Plus remain separate. The résumé labels an iOS link “MedEx,” but it targets MedEx Plus; the README's more precise app identity is used.
- Product-specific technology stacks are not supplied. Global Flutter/architecture/state-management skills are shown in the craft section; project-specific stacks are not guessed.
- The original résumé remains downloadable and unchanged, including its references section. The website itself does not repeat third-party reference contact information.
- This site uses the system font stack, which resolves to SF Pro on supported Apple devices and appropriate fallbacks elsewhere; no proprietary font files are bundled.
